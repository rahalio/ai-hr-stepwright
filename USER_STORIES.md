# Stepwright — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Process owner

- As a process owner, I want the step count and labour hours per transaction for my process measured from live systems against a dated baseline, so that improvement is evidence rather than argument.
- As a process owner, I want to propose a step elimination with the remaining human work described, so that what is left is a designed role rather than whatever survives.
- As a process owner, I want to see which of my steps are rework or reconciliation, so that I attack the causes rather than automating the symptom.
- As a process owner, I want the benefit of my redesign credited to my process when the gate confirms it, so that doing the harder structural work is rewarded over deploying a bot.

### Automation centre-of-excellence lead

- As an automation lead, I want deployments registered against a process and a baseline, so that my portfolio is expressed in productivity terms rather than in bot counts.
- As an automation lead, I want early warning when a deployment's step reduction is not materialising, so that I can fix or retire it inside the quarter instead of defending it at year end.
- As an automation lead, I want assistant authority limits configured and enforced centrally, so that a business unit cannot quietly grant an assistant the ability to change prices.

### Finance business partner

- As a finance business partner, I want to hold forecast benefits as unrealised until a gate passes, so that the budget reflects productivity that has actually happened.
- As a finance business partner, I want shadow capital captured on every benefit case, so that return on automation is calculated against total spend rather than the capitalised fraction.
- As a finance business partner, I want a portfolio view that includes deployments which failed their gate, so that our next investment round is priced on our actual hit rate.

### Internal audit and controls owner

- As a controls owner, I want every assistant-initiated commitment logged with its instruction, limit and reversal status, so that an assistant changing a price is an auditable transaction rather than an anomaly.
- As a controls owner, I want an assistant that breaches its authority limit halted automatically, so that the control is preventive rather than detective.
- As a controls owner, I want segregation-of-duties assertions on every assistant with commitment authority, so that automation does not quietly collapse a control we designed on purpose.

### Workforce planner and HR business partner

- As an HR business partner, I want the workforce impact of each step elimination identified before deployment, so that consultation happens on time rather than retrospectively.
- As a workforce planner, I want the role-mix consequence of the automation portfolio for the analyst, clerk and service-representative populations, so that redeployment and learning capacity is planned rather than improvised.
- As an HR business partner, I want any move of eliminated work to contingent or platform labour to carry a recorded classification assessment, so that a cost saving does not create a co-employment liability.
